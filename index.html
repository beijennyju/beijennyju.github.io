About me

Communication is where movement becomes meaning.

Before joining Manchester, I was an Assistant Professor at Macau University of Science and Technology and a Researcher and Outreach Officer at the United Nations University Institute in Macau.

My work spans cross-border labour migration, transnational family life and international education, with particular attention to women and communities in vulnerable conditions.
